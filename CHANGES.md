## 0.1.0

- Core filter list (CC-BY-4.0) and a cross-browser Manifest V3 extension (AGPL-3.0-or-later).
- Deterministic hiding of known AI surfaces, plus a source-level block (Google `udm=14`) that
  switches the AI off at the URL so it never renders.
- A per-page counter of blocked AI surfaces, with a toolbar badge, a popup that names each surface,
  a lifetime total, and an opt-in on-page count.
- No telemetry, no account required, works on install.
