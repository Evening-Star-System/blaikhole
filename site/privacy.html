Privacy

The short version: Blaikhole collects nothing. No account, no tracking, no analytics, no telemetry. There is no server to send anything to, because we never ask for anything.

What stays on your device

Your settings (which lists are on, your per-site choices) and the running count of AI surfaces blocked are kept in your browser's own local storage. They never leave your machine, and we never see them. Uninstalling Blaikhole removes them.

Network

Blaikhole works offline and makes no calls of its own. The only time it touches the network is:

  • If you add a custom filter-list URL in the options, it fetches that list from the address you chose. That is your call, and nothing about you is sent with the request.
  • Links you click yourself (the source code, the optional account), which open in a new tab like any other link.

Why it needs the permissions it asks for

  • Storage: to save your settings and the blocked count on your device.
  • Access to the pages you visit: to hide AI surfaces and switch AI off at the URL where a site allows it. It reads page structure locally, in your browser, and sends none of it anywhere.
  • Clipboard (write): only when you press Share, to copy a link.

No selling, no sharing, no third parties

There is no advertising, no data broker, no third-party analytics, and nothing is ever sold or shared. The code is open source under the AGPL, so anyone can verify every one of these claims.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the update will be here with a new date. Questions or concerns can be raised on the project on GitHub.
